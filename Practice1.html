B. A composite sample combines material collected across space or time.

Multiple choice1.3 A representative sample should:

Choose one answer for question 1.3
Choose an answer to unlock the reveal.

A. The sample should reflect the population used for the decision.

Written1.4 A fuel spill has visibly stained soil leading toward a ditch. Choose a sampling strategy and explain where samples should be collected.

Write an answer to unlock the reveal.

Use judgmental or biased sampling. Sample the stained soil, the path toward the ditch, and locations just beyond the visible spill.

Written1.5 Explain the difference between constitutional and distributional heterogeneity.

Write an answer to unlock the reveal.

Constitutional heterogeneity is variation between individual particles. Distributional heterogeneity is non-random separation or grouping of particles within the lot.

Lecture 2 · Sampling Statistics

Multiple choice2.1 Fifteen soil results are available. Which mean can be calculated directly?

Choose one answer for question 2.1
Choose an answer to unlock the reveal.

B. Sample mean. The population mean is estimated from the sampled results.

Multiple choice2.2 A 95% confidence interval is 42–50 mg/kg. What does it describe?

Choose one answer for question 2.2
Choose an answer to unlock the reveal.

B. A confidence interval estimates the population mean, not individual results.

Multiple choice2.3 If sample size increases from 10 to 40 and variability stays constant, the standard error becomes approximately:

Choose one answer for question 2.3
Choose an answer to unlock the reveal.

B. SE = s/√n. Increasing n by four reduces SE by two.

Written2.4 Why is the population mean for a soil site estimated instead of measured directly? Give one practical reason.

Write an answer to unlock the reveal.

Measuring it directly would require collecting and analyzing all soil in the defined area and depth. That is impractical because of the amount of material, time, and cost.

Written2.5 A set of 16 results has a standard deviation of 8.0 mg/kg. Calculate the standard error.

Write an answer to unlock the reveal.

SE = s/√n = 8.0/√16 = 2.0 mg/kg.

Lecture 3 · Designing a Sampling Plan

Multiple choice3.1 In simple random sampling:

Choose one answer for question 3.1

A. Selection is made using a random process rather than visual judgment or convenience.

Multiple choice3.2 Which description best matches systematic sampling?

Choose one answer for question 3.2
Choose an answer to unlock the reveal.

A. A regular spacing or interval is used.

Multiple choice3.3 A site has separate sandy and clay-rich zones. Which strategy ensures both zones are sampled?

Choose one answer for question 3.3
Choose an answer to unlock the reveal.

B. Treat each zone as a stratum and collect samples from both.

Written3.4 A field contains an upland area and a low wet area. Choose a sampling strategy that represents both areas and explain it.

Write an answer to unlock the reveal.

Use stratified sampling. Define the upland and wet areas as separate strata, then collect samples from each area.

Written3.5 A decision concerns soil from 0–60 cm, but samples were taken only from 0–15 cm. State the problem and one correction.

Write an answer to unlock the reveal.

The sampled depth does not represent the target population. Add samples from the deeper part of the 0–60 cm interval.

Lecture 4 · Particle Sampling and Review

Multiple choice4.2 If all other factors stay constant, collecting a larger sample mass generally:

Choose one answer for question 4.2
Choose an answer to unlock the reveal.

B. A larger sample includes more particles and generally represents the lot better.

Multiple choice4.3 Why can grinding reduce fundamental sampling error?

Choose one answer for question 4.3
Choose an answer to unlock the reveal.

A. Fundamental sampling error has a strong dependence on particle diameter.

Written4.4 Grinding reduces particle diameter from 5.0 mm to 2.5 mm. Calculate the new required sample mass as a fraction of the original mass.

Write an answer to unlock the reveal.

(2.5/5.0)3 = 0.125 = 1/8 of the original mass.

Written4.5 Give one practical way to reduce fundamental sampling error and explain why it works. (Hint: particle size or sample mass.)

Write an answer to unlock the reveal.

Reduce particle size by grinding, or collect a larger sample mass. Either change helps the sample represent more of the particles in the lot.

Multiple choice5.2 For K = Cs/Cm, a larger K generally means the solute:

Choose one answer for question 5.2

C. A larger distribution coefficient indicates greater preference for the stationary phase.

Multiple choice5.3 A solute has tR = 4.00 min and tM = 1.00 min. What is k′?

Choose one answer for question 5.3
Choose an answer to unlock the reveal.

C. k′ = (4.00 − 1.00)/1.00 = 3.00.

Written5.4 Solutes X, Y, and Z have k′ values of 0.50, 4.0, and 1.5. Place them in elution order and explain.

Write an answer to unlock the reveal.

X, Z, Y. A smaller k′ means less time in the stationary phase and earlier elution.

Written5.5 Two analytes have tM = 1.50 min. A elutes at 4.50 min and B at 7.50 min. Calculate both k′ values and α.

Write an answer to unlock the reveal.

k′A = (4.50 − 1.50)/1.50 = 2.00; k′B = (7.50 − 1.50)/1.50 = 4.00; α = 4.00/2.00 = 2.00.

C. Greater efficiency means larger N, smaller H, and narrower peaks.

Multiple choice6.3 A much larger injection makes a peak broader and shifts its retention time. What should be tried first?

Choose one answer for question 6.3
Choose an answer to unlock the reveal.

B. Reducing the injection helps prevent column overload.

Written6.4 A peak has tR = 5.00 min and baseline width w = 0.25 min on a 20.0 cm column. Calculate N and H in micrometres. Compare with a system having H = 50 μm.

Write an answer to unlock the reveal.

N = 16(5.00/0.25)2 = 6400. H = 20.0 cm/6400 = 0.003125 cm = 31.25 μm. The first system is more efficient because it has the smaller plate height and should give narrower peaks.

Written6.5 A peak has tR = 6.00 min and half-height width w1/2 = 0.15 min. Calculate N.

Write an answer to unlock the reveal.

N = 5.54(6.00/0.15)2 = 5.54(40)2 = 8864.
