Concept overview

Sensitivity is a method's ability to respond reliably and measurably to changes in analyte concentration. Every measurement has noise, and at some point signal can no longer be distinguished from that noise. The limit of detection (LOD) is the lowest concentration that can be detected under the method's conditions. The limit of quantitation (LOQ) is higher than the LOD and should have better precision: it is the lowest concentration that can be measured with a given accuracy and precision.

Today you will estimate LOD and LOQ two ways: from the calibration graph (using the slope and the standard deviation of the y-intercept), and visually from signal-to-noise ratios measured directly on chromatograms. Because you are working close to the detection limit, this lab relaxes its targets a bit compared to Labs A and B: R2 above 0.95, %RSD under 10%, and % recovery within plus or minus 15 of 100%.
